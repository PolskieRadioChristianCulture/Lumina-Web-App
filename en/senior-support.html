If you need help now

For an immediate threat to life or health call 112. Do not wait for Christian Culture to reply.

Domestic violence — Niebieska Linia in Poland: 800 120 002 — free, around the clock. Ask about language availability.

Three first steps

You do not have to solve everything today. Choose one manageable step.

  1. 01

    Ask what is needed

    Do not assume what an older person needs. Ask where they welcome support and what they want to continue doing independently.

  2. 02

    Agree on regular contact

    Choose a suitable way to stay in touch and what to do if someone does not answer. Do not impose monitoring without consent.

  3. 03

    Check municipal support

    Ask the local social assistance centre in Poland about home care or day support, eligibility and charges.

PREPARE FOR A CONVERSATION

Protect against scams

Do not share codes, passwords or money under pressure. End a suspicious call and contact the bank or a trusted person yourself using a known number. Do not install software at a stranger’s request.

Help that respects choice

Help someone understand a document or arrange an appointment without taking over their decisions without a proper basis. Discuss powers of attorney with a lawyer. Financial abuse also calls for action.
