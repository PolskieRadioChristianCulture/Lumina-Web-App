Important: the role of Christian Culture

Christian Culture helps you connect with independent specialists and support providers. Each provider is responsible for their own advice and the way they provide support. A conversation does not guarantee a particular outcome.

Terms of use and scope of responsibility

Christian Culture — Christian Social Initiative provides information and organisational support to facilitate contact with independent specialists, volunteers and support providers. Making contact details available does not itself mean that Christian Culture provides medical treatment, psychotherapy, legal services or financial advice.

Each support provider is responsible for their own statements and recommendations, the conduct of the meeting and working within their qualifications and professional authorisation. The scope and terms of support should be agreed directly with that provider.

Christian Culture does not guarantee a particular outcome and does not assume responsibility for independent professional advice, users’ own decisions or their consequences, subject to any responsibility imposed by applicable law. Users should consider their own circumstances and consult an appropriately qualified professional where professional authorisation is required.

This notice does not exclude Christian Culture’s responsibility for its own acts or omissions, or limit users’ rights under applicable law. Support conversations are not an emergency service or a crisis intervention service.

GUIDE · A FIRST STEP

Keep your documents together.

Organising information helps you describe your situation. This prepares you for a conversation; it is not a set of legal instructions.

  1. Gather letters you have received, agreements and payment confirmations.
  2. Note when documents arrived and any dates mentioned. Do not determine legal consequences yourself based on this guide.
  3. Prepare a short list of questions to ask a qualified professional.
  4. Use official contact channels. Do not send document scans to unfamiliar people.
