If you need help now

For an immediate threat to life or health call 112. Do not wait for Christian Culture to reply.

Adults in a mental health crisis in Poland: 800 70 22 22 — free, around the clock. Ask about language availability.

Three first steps

You do not have to solve everything today. Choose one manageable step.

  1. 01

    Describe the difficulty

    What feels hardest now? When did it start and how does it affect sleep, work or relationships? A few private notes are enough.

  2. 02

    Ask about qualifications

    Check training, scope of practice, confidentiality and fees. Ask what the first consultation involves.

  3. 03

    Check local access

    Adults can look up their local Mental Health Centre and its registration and coordination point in Poland.

PREPARE FOR A CONVERSATION

Your first meeting

You do not have to share everything at once. Ask about the purpose of the conversation, possible next steps and how to end the service. Explanations should be understandable.

Different kinds of care

Psychologists, psychotherapists and psychiatrists have different roles. Ask the service which consultation suits your need. This page does not assess symptoms or select treatment.

Good to know

Do I need a referral?

Requirements depend on the service. Adults can contact their local CZP registration and coordination point without a referral or prior booking. Check the centre’s current arrangements.

Can prayer replace professional care?

No. Optional spiritual support can accompany qualified care and should not lead you to stop prescribed treatment.
