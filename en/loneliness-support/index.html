CC HELP · LONELINESS AND CONNECTION

A small connection can be a good beginning.

Feeling alone deserves care and attention. Find a safe form of connection that fits your circumstances.

Start with one step ↗Where to find help →

Respect for your situation. No judgement and no promises of instant solutions.

If you need help now

For an immediate threat to life or health call 112. Do not wait for Christian Culture to reply.

Adults in a mental health crisis in Poland: 800 70 22 22 — free, around the clock. Ask about language availability.

Sources and service details below ↓

Three first steps

You do not have to solve everything today. Choose one manageable step.

  1. 01

    Name the connection you need

    Would a conversation with someone you know, a shared activity or professional support help? Begin with one need without pressure to build many relationships quickly.

  2. 02

    Suggest one manageable step

    A short phone call, a walk or meeting in a familiar place may be easier than a vague promise to meet someday. Respect your energy and personal boundaries.

  3. 03

    Check safe local opportunities

    Ask a library, cultural centre, local activity centre or familiar faith community about activities. Confirm the organiser, terms and any charges before joining.

PREPARE FOR A CONVERSATION

Relationships at your own pace

You do not have to demonstrate openness by sharing private experiences or accepting every invitation. One person’s lack of response is not a measure of your value. You can choose a different time, form of contact or professional support.

Boundaries online

Do not send money, login codes or documents to an online acquaintance under emotional pressure. Loneliness does not oblige you to trust a stranger. If suffering persists or everyday life becomes difficult, ask a qualified professional about support.

Good to know

Can a chatbot replace someone close to me?

AI is a tool, not a human or a partner. We do not present chat as a substitute for a mutual relationship or qualified support.

Must I attend a religious activity to seek help?

No. Spiritual support is optional. You may use appropriate secular services, activities and professional care while respecting your own beliefs.
