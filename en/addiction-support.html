If you need help now

For an immediate threat to life or health call 112. Do not wait for Christian Culture to reply.

Adults in a mental health crisis in Poland: 800 70 22 22 — free, around the clock. Ask about language availability.

Three first steps

You do not have to solve everything today. Choose one manageable step.

  1. 01

    Name the need

    Note what is troubling you and how it affects daily life. You do not need to diagnose yourself.

  2. 02

    Find a consultation

    Use KCPU’s information for Poland. Ask the service about an initial appointment, admission requirements and fees.

  3. 03

    Choose a safe step

    Ask someone you trust to help arrange a conversation. Seek medical help if health or life is at risk.

PREPARE FOR A CONVERSATION

If you need support

Ask about treatment options, confidentiality and the provider’s qualifications. Do not change treatment or plan substance withdrawal based on internet advice.

If you support someone else

You may seek help for yourself even if your loved one is not ready. Discuss specific behaviours and your boundaries. You are not responsible for another person’s decisions.

Christian Culture facilitates contact with independent professionals and support providers. Each provider is responsible for their own advice and service. Taking part in a conversation does not guarantee any particular outcome.
