Three first steps

You do not have to solve everything today. Choose one manageable step.

  1. 01

    Notice a specific moment

    Record when the difficulty occurs, what comes before it and what the child may need. Do not assume bad intentions.

  2. 02

    Listen to the child

    Ask in age-appropriate words how they see the situation. Allow an answer without shaming.

  3. 03

    Find support

    A school psychologist or educator can be a starting point. Ask about the appropriate local counselling service.

PREPARE FOR A CONVERSATION

Boundaries without humiliation

Explain rules and their purpose in words the child understands. Avoid violence, threats and public shaming. When adult emotions rise, arrange a safe pause.

When a difficulty persists

If you are worried about a child’s functioning or safety, speak with a qualified professional. Do not diagnose from a video or online quiz. Children have a right to respectful treatment and help.

Can a child contact someone directly?

In Poland the free 800 12 12 12 children’s helpline and czat.brpd.gov.pl are available around the clock. The chat requires no login; ask about language availability.
