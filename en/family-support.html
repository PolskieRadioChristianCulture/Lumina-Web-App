Care for loved ones. Remember yourself.

Clarify your family’s needs and find the right help without placing every responsibility on one person.

If you need help now

For an immediate threat to life or health call 112. Do not wait for Christian Culture to reply.

Domestic violence — Niebieska Linia in Poland: 800 120 002 — free, around the clock. Ask about language availability.

Three first steps

You do not have to solve everything today. Choose one manageable step.

  1. 01

    Name the urgent need

    Is the issue care, conflict, money or safety? Choose the most pressing concern instead of solving everything at once.

  2. 02

    Share responsibility

    If it is safe, agree on specific tasks and each person’s capacity. A child should not act as a mediator between adults.

  3. 03

    Check local assistance

    Ask your municipality’s social assistance centre in Poland about available support and eligibility.

PREPARE FOR A CONVERSATION

A plan for the next week

Agree on one task, who will take it and when to review whether it helps. Include rest for carers and respect each person’s boundaries.

Safety comes first

Where violence is present, joint mediation is not our recommended first response. Someone experiencing harm can seek individual support without the family’s permission. Call 112 for an immediate threat to life or health.
