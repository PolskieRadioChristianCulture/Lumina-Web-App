Original Faith Testimony

In His Own Words

Supplied directly by Gerald Welch to Christian Culture Network

I'm always willing to share my testimony. It's not any kind of world shattering thing that will resonate across the ages, but it's mine.

I was raised in church. From the time I was a little boy, attending church was simply part of my life. But looking back, I realize that church was more like a club to me than a personal relationship with Jesus Christ. I knew about God, but I didn't truly know Him.

When I was twelve years old, my father died. Over the next three years, my mother moved us sixteen times. We never stayed anywhere long enough for me to build meaningful friendships or feel that I belonged. Eventually, I withdrew into myself. I expressed those feelings in a song I've written for a future album:

My father died when I was twelve
We moved so much I lost myself
Sixteen schools and zero homes
I numbed my heart and walked alone
Excerpt from an original song intended for a future album.

It was during those difficult years that I taught myself to play guitar. Music became something I could carry with me, no matter where we lived.

When we finally settled down three years later, I slowly began letting my guard down. I started attending a local church, and I even rode along on the church bus as we picked people up for services and took them home afterward. I became good friends with the associate pastor, who was only a few years older than I was. He knew about my father's death, the constant moving, and the isolation I had experienced.

One night in August 1981, after we had dropped everyone off, he stopped the bus and made a heartfelt appeal to me. He told me that I was finally home, and that it was time to come to God. Those words reached deep inside me. We prayed together, and I felt the presence of the Holy Spirit. I repented of my sins and surrendered my life to Jesus Christ. It was around midnight, but I didn't want to wait. I asked to be baptized immediately. He took me to a little swimming pool beside his house, and there, in the middle of the night, I was baptized.

I can’t adequately describe what happened inside me. It felt as though the years of grief, bitterness, loneliness, and loss were flowing out of me. For the first time, Christianity wasn't simply something I attended. It was real. Jesus Christ was real to me. My life changed.

I began attending every church service, hungry to learn more about the Word of God. I started playing bass guitar in the church choir and writing songs to praise the Lord. Music was no longer simply something I did for myself. It had become a way to express my faith and share what God was teaching me.

That was more than forty years ago.

My faith today is sustained by the knowledge of what Jesus has done for me and for all mankind. I understand that this earthly life, with all its joys, suffering, accomplishments, and losses, is only a brief moment compared with eternity. That understanding has shaped my songwriting.

I have never wanted to write generic Christian songs simply because they sound religious. Every composition comes from a personal experience, a moment of worship, or a message that has meant something important to me. WORLD WAR YOU grew out of my early experiences as a Christian. I began to understand that Christianity was not a social club. It was a real relationship with a living Savior, and there was a real spiritual battle taking place beyond what our physical eyes could see.

The enemy wants us to fail. Fear, deception, temptation, and discouragement are real challenges. But Christ has not left us defenseless. Scripture calls us to put on the whole armor of God, to stand firm in faith, and to remember that the victory belongs to Him. That’s the message behind WORLD WAR YOU. It’s a song about recognizing the spiritual battle around us and understanding that every soul must make a choice, an active, willing choice.

My upcoming album, SECOND WIND, reflects my musical style of the 1980s, the era in which I wrote these songs. It includes Christian rock, other musical styles, and two instrumental pieces.

People sometimes ask why I include instrumentals on Christian albums. My answer is simple: not every moment of worship needs words. Some of my instrumental compositions were written during moments of prayer, gratitude, or quiet worship. They express what was in my heart at that particular moment, when words were unnecessary.

After decades of writing music, I am finally able to bring many of these compositions to life through modern recording technology. The songs, their melodies, lyrics, and the experiences behind them are my own.

My prayer is that listeners will hear more than a recording. I hope they will recognize something of the faith, gratitude, conviction, and love for Jesus Christ that inspired these songs. And if someone hears WORLD WAR YOU and begins to understand that Christianity is more than a religious institution or a weekly gathering, then perhaps the song will have accomplished something far more important than simply being heard.

Gerald Welch
Grateful Child of the Living God • Christian Songwriter and Composer
