JJ Beauvais: Two Songs of Hope and Worship

Discover Now Is The Time and Written In Heaven (Live), two Christian songs shared with permission by JJ Beauvais.

Christian Culture Global is pleased to share two recordings by JJ Beauvais: Now Is The Time and Written In Heaven (Live).

Now Is The Time calls listeners toward prayer, repentance and renewed faith in Jesus Christ.

Written In Heaven (Live) draws inspiration from Luke 10:20 and the joy of belonging to God.
