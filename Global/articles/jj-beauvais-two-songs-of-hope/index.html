Artist Story: JJ Beauvais — A Life of Worship

Discover Now Is The Time and Written In Heaven (Live), two Christian songs shared with permission by JJ Beauvais.

Christian Culture Global is pleased to share two recordings by JJ Beauvais: Now Is The Time and Written In Heaven (Live).

Now Is The Time calls listeners toward prayer, repentance and renewed faith in Jesus Christ, reminding us of the urgency of the Gospel.

Written In Heaven (Live) draws inspiration from Luke 10:20 and the eternal joy of belonging to God through Christ.

Both recordings are shared with the artist’s non-exclusive permission, with attribution. Follow the links below to watch on YouTube.
