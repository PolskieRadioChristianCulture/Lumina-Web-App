Bible in Music · 2026-10-16

Biblical Worship — Sing the Entire Word of God

A journey through the 66 books and 1,189 chapters of Scripture — a vision for music, learning and worship.

Music has long been a way to remember, celebrate and reflect on Scripture. The Psalms show how song can carry prayer, praise and lament across generations.

Christian Culture’s Biblical Worship initiative aims to create musical adaptations across the 66 books and 1,189 chapters of the Protestant Bible. It is a long-term goal, not a completed catalog.

The initiative also envisions synchronized verse-by-verse karaoke, so families and communities can sing along with the text. Availability must be verified on the project website before claiming the feature is live.

Musical adaptation should respect the meaning and literary context of Scripture, and clearly identify the translation and whether the lyrics are verbatim or adapted.

Discover the initiative at polskieradio.cc/BiblijneUwielbienie.
