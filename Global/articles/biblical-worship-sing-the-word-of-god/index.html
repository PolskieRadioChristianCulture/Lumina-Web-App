Bible in Music · 2026-10-16

Biblical Worship — Sing the Entire Word of God!

A journey through the 66 books and 1,189 chapters of Scripture — a vision for music, learning and worship.

Music has long been a way to remember, celebrate and reflect on Scripture. The Psalms show how song can carry prayer, praise and lament across generations.

Christian Culture’s Biblical Worship initiative aims to create musical adaptations across the 66 books and 1,189 chapters of the Protestant Bible. It is a monumental journey of faith and craft.

The initiative also features synchronized word-by-word karaoke, so families and communities can sing along with the exact biblical text in true harmony.

Musical adaptation strictly respects the sacred meaning and context of Scripture, clearly identifying the translation and keeping fidelity to God’s holy Word.

Discover and experience the initiative at polskieradio.cc/BiblijneUwielbienie.
